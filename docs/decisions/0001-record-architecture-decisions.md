# 1. Record architecture decisions

Date: 2026-10-21

## Status

Accepted

## Context

0-0 will have contributors who were not present for early choices. Decisions about the cue format and the core architecture are expensive to reverse, so the reasons behind them should be written down.

## Decision

We keep short architecture decision records in `docs/decisions/`, numbered in order, with Context, Decision and Consequences sections. A superseded record stays in place and links to its replacement.

## Consequences

Significant changes, especially to the cue format, come with a decision record in the same pull request.
